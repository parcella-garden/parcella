# Getting started

Welcome to Parcella. This section is for you if you have just joined the
board, or if your club has just installed Parcella and you are the
person setting it up.

## What Parcella is

Parcella is the board's shared workspace for running an allotment
garden association. Instead of a folder of spreadsheets, a box of meter
cards and a member list on somebody's laptop, everything lives in one
place that every board member can open in a web browser:

- **Members and parcels** -- who belongs to the club, who leases which
  parcel, and the full history of both.
- **Club life** -- work sessions and work hours, the calendar,
  announcements, a task board, notes and step-by-step processes.
- **Money and supplies** -- water and electricity meters, insurance,
  annual invoices, purchase requests that need two approvals, and the
  club's inventory.
- **Contact with the outside world** -- applicants for a free plot,
  suppliers and authorities, and the support inbox.

Each of these areas is a *module*. Your club switches on only the
modules it actually uses, so your screen may show fewer entries than
this guide describes. See [Modules](../modules/index.md) for the full
list.

!!! note "Gardeners don't log in"
    Parcella is for the board and the people who help run the club.
    Ordinary gardeners have no account. They see at most a form on the
    club website, for example to sign up for a work session -- see
    [For gardeners](../gardeners/index.md).

## Who can see what

Every person who logs in has their own account. What an account can
see and change depends on the **groups** it belongs to: a group can
give full access to everything, or only, say, read and write access to
the water meters. The administrators of your installation set this up.
[Roles, groups and permissions](roles-and-permissions.md) explains it
in detail.

## Setting up a new installation?

If you are the person who installed Parcella, start with
[First login](first-login.md#the-very-first-login-after-installation) --
the built-in administrator account must get a new password before
anything else works. Then follow the task guide
[Setting up a new club](../guides/new-club-setup.md), and look through

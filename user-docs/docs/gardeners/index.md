# For gardeners: signing up for a work session

This page is for gardeners of a club that uses Parcella. Your board
can link to it from the club website.

!!! info "In short"
    You don't need an account or a password. You sign up on your
    club's website with your **parcel number**. The board then sees
    your sign-up, and you get a confirmation e-mail.

## Where to sign up

Your club's website has a page with a sign-up form for work sessions
(sometimes called "Arbeitseinsatz" or "community work"). Ask your board
where to find it if you're not sure.

The form only lists sessions that:

- haven't taken place yet,
- still have free places (the number of places left may be shown next
  to the date), and
- are still open for sign-up -- some sessions close a day or more
  before they start.

If a session you expected is missing, it is probably full or closed.
Contact your board directly.

## Filling in the form

The exact look depends on your club's website. Usually you see:

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Optional, but helpful: your name as the club knows it. Important if several people share your parcel. |
| **Parcel number** | Required. Choose your parcel number from the list. |
| Sessions | Tick one or more sessions you want to join. |
| **Remarks** | Optional. Anything the board should know, e.g. "I can only come until noon". |

Then click **Sign up**.

!!! note "Why only a parcel number?"
    The website never shows who leases which parcel -- that's private.
    Parcella works out who you are from the parcel number and the name
    you type.

## What happens next

You see a message on the website:

- **Thank you, your signup has been received.** -- all done.
- If some of the ticked sessions were full or closed in the meantime,
  the message says so. The other sessions are booked.
- If something went wrong (unknown parcel number, no session ticked,
  too many attempts in a short time), the message tells you what to do.

Behind the scenes:

- If your name matches one of the people on your parcel, **only you**
  are signed up.
- If you left the name empty, or it doesn't clearly match, **everybody
  on your parcel** is signed up, and the board is asked to check. Don't
  worry -- the board removes whoever isn't coming.
- Signing up twice for the same session doesn't create a double entry.

## The e-mails you receive

After a successful sign-up, each person who was signed up gets a
**confirmation e-mail**, one per session. The subject reads
"RE: your registration to work session on" followed by the date (in
your club's language). It contains a short text from your board and
the session's title, date and time. If only you were signed up, your
remarks are quoted too.

Good to know:

- The e-mail goes to the address **the club has on file for you**, not
  to any address typed into the form. If you don't receive it, the club
  may not have your current e-mail address -- tell the board.
- If nobody on your parcel has an e-mail address on file, nobody gets
  a confirmation. The sign-up still counts.
- Your board can switch confirmation e-mails off.

There is no e-mail to cancel. **To cancel or change a sign-up, contact
your board** -- by phone, e-mail or the club's contact form.

## Other forms on your club's website

Your club may use Parcella for other website forms too:

- **Contact form** -- your message goes straight into the board's
  support inbox. You get the answer by e-mail from there.
- **Plot application** -- for people who would like a garden. The
  board sees your application and contacts you.

## Questions?

Contact your club's board. This guide is general; your club's website
and its rules for work sessions come first.

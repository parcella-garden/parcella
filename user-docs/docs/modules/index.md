# Modules

Parcella is made of modules: one per area of club work. Your club
switches on only the ones it needs; the others disappear from the
navigation for everybody. Switching a module off never deletes its
data -- switch it on again and everything is back.

An administrator switches modules on and off under
[Turning modules on and off](../administration/modules.md). Who may
use a switched-on module is set with
[groups](../administration/groups.md).

## Overview

| Module | What it's for | On after installation? |
|---|---|---|
| [Members](members.md) | Everybody who belongs to the club: contact details, membership dates, roles, history. | Always on |
| [Parcels](parcels.md) | The garden plots, their leases and tenants, past and present. | Always on |
| [Applicants](applicants.md) | People who applied for a free plot, their status and the board's notes. | Yes |
| [Work hours](work-hours.md) | Work sessions, sign-ups and attendance, sponsorships, hours transfers and the annual evaluation of required hours. | Yes |
| [Water and electricity](metering.md) | Meters, readings and consumption reports. Water and electricity are switched on separately. | Yes |
| [Insurance](insurance.md) | Property and accident insurance per parcel and year, with an annual report. | Yes |
| [Finances](finances/index.md) | Annual invoices per parcel, payments, reminders, bank accounts, incoming invoices and bookkeeping. | No |
| [Purchase requests](purchase-requests.md) | Club expenses approved by two board members before money is spent. | Yes |
| [Calendar](calendar.md) | Community calendar, birthdays, board presence and absence, your own calendars -- each also as a subscribable calendar feed. | Yes |
| [Announcements](announcements.md) | Write club news once and send it to the website, by e-mail and as a printable notice. | No |
| [Task board](tasks.md) | A To Do / In Progress / Done board for club business. Board and administrators only. | Yes |
| [Inventory](inventory.md) | Everything the club owns (and members' items kept on club ground), with lending. | Yes |
| [Notes](notes.md) | Meeting minutes and other notes, with categories and tags, optionally kept as files in the cloud. | Yes |
| [Processes](processes.md) | Your own step-by-step procedures, such as a parcel handover, run for a parcel, member or applicant. | Yes |
| [External contacts](external-contacts.md) | Suppliers, craftsmen and authorities, linked to invoices and purchase requests. | Yes |
| [Support](support.md) | Your club's helpdesk conversations, linked to members, parcels and tasks. | No |
| [Reporting](reporting.md) | Club figures over the years as charts. Read-only. | Yes |

## Why some modules start switched off

Modules that are off after installation each do something a club
should decide on consciously:

- **Finances** handles members' money and sends invoices in bulk.
- **Announcements** can e-mail every member and post to your website.
- **Support** connects to an outside helpdesk and needs its access
  details.

The same goes for the add-ons in the module list that have no chapter
of their own: **Cloud storage**, **Nextcloud Deck Sync**, **MCP
connector for AI clients**, and the three public website forms
(**Public signup API**, **Public contact-form API**, **Public
applicant-form API**). They either store passwords for outside
services or open a form to the public internet. See
[Turning modules on and off](../administration/modules.md) and
[Integrations](../administration/integrations.md).
